Past Events

Pongal

Pongal is a vibrant, multi-day Hindu harvest festival primarily celebrated by the Tamil community to thank the Sun God and nature for a bountiful harvest. Occurring every mid-January at the start of the Tamil month Thai, it aligns with the winter solstice and the Sun's northward journey (Uttarayan).

Siva Rathri

Maha Shivaratri ("The Great Night of Shiva") is a major Hindu festival celebrated annually in reverence of Lord Shiva. Observed on the 14th night of the waning moon in the lunar month of Phalguna (Feb–March), it symbolizes the triumph of light over darkness and marks the divine union of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati.
